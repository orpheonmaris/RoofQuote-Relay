THE SYSTEM

What happens when a lead comes in.

Four simple steps for incoming website estimate requests, including on your busiest weeks.

01 / INTAKE
⌁

The lead comes in

Homeowners use a simple estimate form, or we connect an approved integration to the form already on your site. You see the request details you need to respond.

02 / RESPONSE
✉

The homeowner hears back

A short confirmation lets them know their request arrived. It doesn't promise a callback time or an appointment.

03 / HANDOFF
↗

You get the alert

Your team receives the new inquiry and sees it in a shared status list, ready for a real person to follow up.

04 / FOLLOW-THROUGH
◷

Nobody gets forgotten

Optional staff reminders flag inquiries still unhandled. A consent-based homeowner check-in can also be configured; you get a weekly activity summary.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Good questions before you start.

Does this replace my CRM?

No. We start with a simple lead-status tracker and one approved website form. An existing CRM integration needs separate assessment and scope.

Will it book estimates or answer calls?

No. It sends receipt acknowledgements, alerts staff and tracks follow-up. Appointments, phone calls and estimates still require your team.

What about urgent leaks?

Your own phone line remains the emergency channel. This is not an emergency-dispatch or voicemail service.

What happens after the 14-day pilot?

We review inquiry-handling data together. If you want to continue, we agree on a managed plan starting at $750/month for the defined scope. If not, there is no automatic ongoing subscription.

What would you need from me to set it up?

You approve access to one website inquiry source, identify who should receive notifications, and help test the acknowledgements and statuses. Never send passwords by email; we'll use delegated access or a client-controlled account where supported.

Do I need Google Workspace?

The first version uses a Google account you control for Forms, Sheets and Apps Script. A free Google account may be sufficient for a modest pilot; a paid Workspace plan is not automatically required. We'll check volume limits, privacy and access needs first.

Who controls customer data?

Your team controls its Google account and the customer-inquiry records there. We agree on access, retention and offboarding before going live.

Do you guarantee new roofing jobs?

No. The pilot measures whether requests are acknowledged, routed and acted on. Better visibility isn't proof of additional sales.
