RoofQuote Relay / roofing-company setup

We'll handle the setup. You keep control.

This page is for roofing companies beginning an agreed RoofQuote Relay pilot. Share a few business details so we can prepare your own private RoofQuote Relay workspace. You will manage inquiries through our web application; no Google account or coding is required. We activate live intake only after it has been configured, tested, and approved.

1. Share business detailsProvide a contact, the owner's preferred sign-in email, and your current intake process.
2. Set up your private accountAfter email delivery is verified, the authorized owner signs in using a one-time email link. We never need your password.
3. Approve a tested pilotWe test with fictional information, show you the tracker, and launch only after separate written sign-off.

Tell us about your company

This is a business setup questionnaire, not a homeowner roof-estimate form. No payment is collected here, and sending it does not authorize live emails or start a subscription.

Formspree processes this business questionnaire and can email it to RoofQuote Relay. See our privacy notice.

What happens after you send this?

  1. We review your company's current form and email setup. If your CRM already sends confirmations, we avoid duplicate messages.
  2. We agree on the exact one-source pilot scope, billing terms, privacy controls, and who handles follow-ups.
  3. After we verify the private workspace and email delivery, the designated owner receives a secure sign-in invitation. You never share passwords or verification codes.
  4. We test fictional inquiries in the private dashboard, verify alerts and status tracking, and show staff how to use the system. No real homeowner is contacted during testing.
  5. Only after written go-live approval and successful delivery tests do we activate your branded intake form and transactional acknowledgments.
Proposed pilot deliverables: a branded inquiry form, private web dashboard, status tracking, owner alerts, acknowledgments after approval, internal overdue reminders, and factual operational reports. We confirm technical readiness before accepting homeowner information.

What isn't included

We do not generate roofing leads, answer calls, book inspections, send SMS, or automatically monitor your existing CRM. Existing website-form integrations require a separate technical review.

Account protection: We will never ask you for passwords, sign-in codes or links, private keys, or administrator credentials. If someone does, stop and contact us directly.
