RoofQuote Relay / roofing-company setup

We'll handle the setup. You keep control.

This page is for roofing companies beginning an agreed RoofQuote Relay pilot. Send us a few business details and we will prepare a limited, client-owned Google Forms and Sheets installation. You don't need to write or edit code.

1. Share business detailsProvide a contact, a Google account email you own, and your current intake process.
2. Authorize GoogleDuring a guided setup, review and approve the permissions yourself. We never need your password.
3. Approve a tested pilotWe test with fictional information, show you the tracker, and launch only after separate written sign-off.

Tell us about your company

This is a business setup questionnaire, not a homeowner roof-estimate form. No payment is collected here, and sending it does not authorize live emails or start a subscription.

Formspree processes this business questionnaire and can email it to RoofQuote Relay. See our privacy notice.

What happens after you send this?

  1. We review your company's current form and email setup. If your CRM already sends confirmations, we avoid duplicate messages.
  2. We agree on the exact one-source pilot scope, billing terms, privacy controls, and who handles follow-ups.
  3. We schedule an owner-assisted Google account setup. You stay signed in and authorize the application permissions; you do not share credentials.
  4. We test two fictional inquiries, confirm alerts and status tracking, and show you how to use the sheet. Real homeowners are not emailed in TEST mode.
  5. Only after written go-live approval do we publish the client intake URL and activate transactional acknowledgments.
What you receive: a Google Form, private Google Sheets lead tracker, owner alerts, automatic receipt confirmations after approval, optional internal overdue reminders, and a weekly factual report.

What isn't included

We do not generate roofing leads, answer calls, book inspections, send SMS, or automatically monitor your existing CRM. Existing website-form integrations require a separate technical review.

Account protection: We will never ask you for your Google password, one-time verification code, private keys, or administrator login. If someone does, stop and contact us directly.
