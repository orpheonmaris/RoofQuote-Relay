YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Effective November 17, 2026. RoofQuote Relay is an independent, early-stage service for roofing business operators. Questions about privacy can be emailed to roofquoterelay@outlook.com.

What this site collects

The interactive demonstration runs in your browser without sending sample inputs to our servers. When you submit the business inquiry form, your browser sends your name, company, email, how inquiries arrive, and any optional message, business phone number, or preferred callback time directly to Formspree for storage, spam filtering, and email notification. Please do not send homeowner details, residential addresses, project photos, or private customer information through this form.

Roofing-company pilot onboarding

The optional client onboarding questionnaire also collects business-only setup information: the authorized representative's identity, company name and office contact, a company-controlled Google account email address, website and privacy-policy links if provided, approximate inquiry volume, time zone, existing email acknowledgments, and workflow preferences. These details are submitted directly to Formspree so we can assess and prepare an agreed pilot. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, homeowner records or payment details. Completing the questionnaire does not authorize live homeowner messaging or a subscription.

Our unlisted operator installer page generates a company-specific script entirely inside the operator's browser, using a generic template served from this website. The filled company details are not submitted to RoofQuote Relay by that page; the result is stored locally only if the operator downloads or copies it. The page is not password-protected, so do not enter confidential materials.

Purpose and legal basis

We use the information to respond to your business inquiry, arrange an optional human callback if you requested one, discuss a pilot, and administer a relationship if you choose to become a customer. A form submission does not give consent for unrelated marketing, automated calls, or third-party lead sales.

Providers and storage

The site is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. The business inquiry form submits directly from your browser to Formspree, which processes messages, provides automated spam filtering, and may email notifications to roofquoterelay@outlook.com through Microsoft Outlook. Forms marked as spam may not trigger an email notification. These providers process relevant information under their own policies.

Sharing, security and retention

We do not sell business inquiry data. Authorized providers may handle it for hosting and email. We limit access and keep records only as reasonably needed for inquiries, business administration, and legal obligations. Email isn't a secure place for homeowner or other sensitive information.

Your choices and changes

You can request access, corrections, or deletion where applicable by emailing roofquoterelay@outlook.com, subject to legal obligations. The site does not intentionally run advertising pixels or analytics tracking. We will revise this notice if those practices change.
