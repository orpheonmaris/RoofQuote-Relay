# Welcome to RoofQuote Relay

## Your 14-day roofing inquiry pilot — without technical work

RoofQuote Relay helps your team acknowledge *new website estimate requests*, see what needs a callback, and avoid leaving inquiries untracked. **We handle installation. You keep control of your Google account and customer information.**

### What you need to provide

- Your roofing company's actual business name and website (if applicable).
- A real office phone number and a business email someone monitors.
- The name of the person who will contact homeowners and update the lead list.
- A **Google account your company controls** (Gmail or Workspace) that can use Google Forms, Sheets, and Apps Script.
- Your company's privacy notice, and permission to use a **new company-owned Google Form** as the pilot's single intake source.
- Confirmation of whether your existing website or CRM already automatically acknowledges requests.

Send the business-only setup questionnaire here:

**https://roofquote-relay.pages.dev/pilot-start**

**Do not send:** passwords, two-factor codes, payment card numbers, homeowner details, insurance numbers, customer records, or private login links.

### What happens during our guided setup

1. **We prepare your files.** RoofQuote Relay builds a company-specific TEST-mode installer. You do not write or edit JavaScript.
2. **You authorize your account.** During a supervised session, you sign in to your own Google account and review the Google permissions before accepting them. We never need your password. You may need to paste a prepared script and click *Run*; we'll guide you step by step.
3. **We test the service together.** We submit fictional requests and confirm your private lead list, owner alerts, test acknowledgments, status changes, and weekly reporting. No real homeowners receive messages in TEST mode.
4. **You approve launch separately.** You review the real company identity, privacy notice, sender address, email copy, and operating responsibilities. Nothing goes live until your authorized representative gives written permission and the system passes live-account checks.
5. **You own the result.** The Google Form, private Sheet, script, and outgoing email account remain under the company's control. You can pause the automation, revoke access, export data, or decline further paid service subject to the agreed terms.

### What your team actually does each day

Open the private **Leads** spreadsheet when an alert arrives. Contact the homeowner by phone or email. Change the row from **New** to **Contacted**; change it to **Booked** only after a genuine appointment is agreed. If a homeowner requests no further contact, choose **Do not contact**. The system does not make phone calls or bookings for you.

You can also receive a small number of internal owner reminders and a weekly count of logged requests and staff statuses.

### Scope and costs

The **proposed** pilot is **$250 for 14 days**, with no automatic continuation; the exact scope and payment terms must be agreed first. It covers **one newly created Google Form** for the company, a private tracker, factual receipt emails after live approval, owner alerts, up to two internal reminders, and a weekly activity report. Existing CRM and website-form integration, SMS, phone answering, appointment booking, advertising, and guaranteed new jobs are **not included** unless separately agreed.

We never represent a test or an email status as proof of a completed roofing sale. The system records what your team enters.

### Privacy and help

Your company controls permission to view homeowner submissions. Google Forms, Sheets, and the mailboxes you approve will process the data. Your company must have an appropriate privacy notice and retention process. If something looks wrong, stop using the customer form and email **roofquoterelay@outlook.com**.

**Before your appointment:** Have your Google login available *to use privately yourself*, a monitored business email, business phone, and privacy notice. That's all you need; no coding knowledge required.
