# Client onboarding email templates

These are **drafts**. Do not send until the company has expressed interest, the actual pilot scope has been discussed, and you have a legitimate basis for contacting the recipient. Never insert invented phone numbers, domains or testimonials.

## 1. After a roofing company agrees to discuss a pilot

**Subject:** RoofQuote Relay — next steps for your roofing inquiry pilot

Hello [Contact first name],

Thanks for speaking with me about how [Company] handles website estimate requests.

I'll take care of the technical setup. You won't need to learn programming or purchase new hosting. The system would run in a Google account your company controls, and we'd start with a limited test before sending anything to homeowners.

To prepare your setup, please complete this short business questionnaire:

https://roofquote-relay.pages.dev/pilot-start

It asks for your company name, monitored business email, office phone, company-owned Google account email, and current inquiry process. Please don't send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or homeowner records.

After reviewing the information, we'll confirm the exact scope, the proposed $250 / 14-day pilot terms, and a time to walk through the account permissions together. Nothing automatically activates or renews from submitting the form.

Regards,
RoofQuote Relay
roofquoterelay@outlook.com
