# First roofing client — setup information to obtain once a pilot is agreed

**Recommended no-code intake:** send the authorized company contact to https://roofquote-relay.pages.dev/pilot-start, then use the [operator-assisted setup procedure](../onboarding/OPERATOR_RUNBOOK.md) and the [client guide](../onboarding/CLIENT_GUIDE.md). The public onboarding page collects business-only details through the existing Formspree account; submitting it is not a contract or live-messaging approval. Maintain signed records in a private client workspace.

Do not fill this file with private information in public GitHub. Copy it into the client's private workspace and review it with the actual owner.

- **Company identity:** registered or trade name, legitimate website, service territory, office telephone, business hours, privacy notice URL.
- **Decision owner:** who authorizes the pilot and outgoing transactional email wording.
- **Google owner:** which account owns the Form, Sheet, Apps Script, and the send-mail quota. Verify recovery/2FA and Workspace limits.
- **Intake:** one Google Form to embed/link, or discuss a separately scoped existing-form integration. Avoid having two overlapping acknowledgments if a prior CRM sends one already.
- **Staff handling:** who reads alerts and updates the tracker, whether the owner monitors in the evening/weekend, and urgent-leak escalation.
- **Pilot limits:** actual weekly lead volume, necessary data fields, what counts as Contacted or Booked, time intervals for internal reminders, and expected weekly reports.
- **Data policy:** retention schedule, Google account permissions, incident contact, response-to-opt-out procedure, how to export/delete on cancellation.
- **Financial terms:** pilot price and invoice, no auto-renewal, any optional changes and scope exclusions.
- **Acceptance:** one test sample, one acknowledgement to owner in TEST, one owner alert, status-update test, reminder test, client sign-off before real addresses.

**Never request the client's Google password or ask them to add homeowner details to the public RoofQuote Relay sales website.**
