# RoofQuote Relay — proposed paid pilot scope

## The exact deliverable

A **14-day, single-company, one-intake-source workflow**. The initial source is a Google Form owned by the roofing company; staff can link or embed it on a client-approved page. A separate existing-website form or CRM integration needs its own technical assessment.

### Included in proposed $250 pilot

1. Setup and owner-approved wording for one client-owned Google Form.
2. A Google Sheets tracker with status dropdown, summary dashboard, and operational event log.
3. An immediate owner email for new requests and an automatic homeowner acknowledgment after live approval.
4. Two possible internal reminders for leads that remain New.
5. A weekly factual operational report and daily failure/trigger checks.
6. A setup handoff and a brief follow-up check during the pilot.

### Not included

Inbound marketing or lead acquisition, ads, phone answering, appointment booking, texting/SMS, CRM integration, live phone status detection, multi-inbox reply monitoring, an AI voice agent, guaranteed job wins, guaranteed response times, 24/7 support, automatic homeowner follow-ups, or license/insurance verification. The Google account and any Workspace subscription remain owned and paid for by the client. All customer communications require actual account testing and approval.

### Possible ongoing engagement (not automatically billed)

A separately agreed monthly maintenance service **starting from a proposed $750/month**, after the pilot demonstrates value. Define specific support hours, change limits, alert monitoring, maintenance responsibilities, and cancellation terms before charging. The stated price is an offer to validate, not evidence of willingness to pay.

## Go/no-go rule

If the customer cannot supply a usable Google account, monitored owner email, real company contact information, privacy notice, and named staff responsibility for status updates, **do not activate** the service. The installed test mode is not a substitute for signed launch approval.
