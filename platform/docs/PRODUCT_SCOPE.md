# Product and pricing scope

RoofQuote Relay v1 is a recurring operational service, not a lead-generation agency. It is intended to support a 14-day paid pilot (previously proposed at $250; validate willingness to pay) and a separately agreed managed service (previously proposed starting at $750/month). Neither price nor any financial outcome is guaranteed.

## Included technical modules

- Staff-facing browser dashboard and role-gated access.
- One branded web intake form per company, approval-controlled.
- Owner email notification, one homeowner receipt, staff status tracking.
- Two capped internal reminders and weekly summary.
- Scoped exports, service health flags and logs.

## Not included yet

- Integrations with an existing site/CRM (except using the new form link), phone line, SMS, instant bookings, paid ads, real-time conversation transcripts, payments/invoicing, customer portal, guaranteed callbacks or 24-hour staffed support.
- No promise that acknowledgments stop homeowners from contacting other roofers.

An operator must establish policies for verified sending identity, privacy retention, incident notification, actual support hours, monthly change limits and staff permissions before signing clients.
