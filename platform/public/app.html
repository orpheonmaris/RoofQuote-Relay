Automation workflow

Built-in rules
  1. Homeowner submits request

    The public form captures a non-emergency roofing request.

  2. Owner alert and acknowledgment

    Once live, transactional emails are queued and attempted through your verified sender.

  3. Internal reminders

    For leads marked New: first after 45 minutes, then after 120 more. A maximum of two staff reminders.

  4. Weekly operations report

    Every Monday, a report summarizes recorded statuses and outstanding issues.

Delivery exceptions
